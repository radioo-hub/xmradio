About SiriusXM

America's leading satellite radio broadcaster.

Who We Are

SiriusXM is the premier satellite radio provider in the United States, delivering over 150 channels of commercial-free music, live sports, talk, news, and entertainment to millions of listeners nationwide.

With broadcasting studios in New York City and satellite transmission across North America, we bring the best audio content to cars, homes, and mobile devices throughout the country.

📻 Our Mission

To deliver premium audio entertainment and information to every corner of America, connecting listeners with the content they love, wherever they are.

Our Broadcasting Network

We operate a state-of-the-art satellite broadcasting network with redundant uplink facilities in New York, Los Angeles, and Nashville, ensuring uninterrupted service across all 50 states.

  • Satellite Coverage: 100% of the continental United States
  • Studio Locations: New York, Los Angeles, Nashville
  • Channels: 150+ commercial-free channels
  • Partners: All major automakers
